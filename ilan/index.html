עץ שנפתח ממךאבות למעלה, צאצאים למטה, והקרבה אליך לכל אדם.
תאריכים עבריים ויארצייטשני הלוחות, ותזכורת בערב שלפני.

Ilan Yuchasin: a private Jewish family tree

Import your tree from MyHeritage, Geni or JewishGen, explore it from yourself up and down, keep Hebrew dates and yahrzeits with reminders, and print a beautiful family book. Everything stays on your phone: no cloud, no account. Free for up to 150 people; Premium (monthly, yearly or lifetime) adds unlimited people and a clean book. Works in English and Hebrew.
